## Remaining defects (new queue/packaging only)

**D1, confirmed by reading.** The queue's admission stop meters from the unit base, not the ship base: `measureSize(c.lane, c.base)` at `queue.ts:226`, and `Contract` has no ship-base field (`:13-15`). The authoritative meter in `release.md:4-19` measures from `SHIP_BASE_FILE`, which spans stacked checkpoints and pre-unit unshipped LOC. When unit base differs from ship base, the queue admits past the real cap and the overage is caught only post-integration by the unit script's meter. Fix: carry `shipBase` in the contract and meter from it.

**D2, confirmed by reading.** `bind` cannot succeed while a `complete` holds the lock. The lock is a zero-wait `mkdir` (`:66-70`, `:190-194`) and `complete` holds it across worker and integration checks (up to 600 s each, 32 each). The instructions require binding "immediately" after launch (`continuous-queue.md:105`), treat any nonzero exit as blocking that action (`:18`), and a job never bound can neither complete (`queue.ts:200`) nor be recovered (`continuous-queue.md:128`). Step 4's "fill slots while unrelated workers continue" (`:113`) is therefore only safe if the host never overlaps `complete` with `next`/`bind`, which the text does not say. The error also conflates busy with orphan. Fix: bounded retry for `bind`/`next`/`status`, or a distinct `queue_busy` reason with the writer pid.
